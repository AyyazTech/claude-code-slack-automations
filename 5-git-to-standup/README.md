# 5. Standup From Git

Never write a standup again. Claude reads your last 24 hours of GitHub commits + PRs, drafts your standup in YOUR voice, and posts it directly to `#standup` as you.

📺 **See it in action:** [Watch the AyyazTech tutorial](https://youtube.com/@AyyazTech)

---

## When to Use

- Daily morning standup (you commit → standup writes itself)
- Async-first teams where standups are written, not spoken
- Mondays after a long weekend ("what did I do Friday?")
- When you forget what you worked on yesterday

## Saves

~10 minutes per day. Multiplies if your team has 10 people doing this.

## Prompt

See [`prompt.md`](./prompt.md) — copy-paste into your `#standup` channel.

## Routing

Routes to **Code mode** automatically.

## Requirements

- `@Claude` invited to `#standup` channel
- Claude GitHub App installed on the target repo
- Repo connected at claude.ai/code
- Your commits authored under YOUR git email (so Claude can filter by author)

## Output

- 3-bullet standup posted directly in `#standup` channel
- Format: Shipped / In Progress / Blockers
- Written in your voice based on your actual commit messages
